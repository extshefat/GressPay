# Earth land mask

`earth-land.png` is a 720×360 grayscale equirectangular mask (white = land, black = ocean).

Source polygons: Natural Earth 1:110m land, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
GeoJSON mirror used to rasterize this file: https://github.com/martynafford/natural-earth-geojson (`110m/physical/ne_110m_land.json`).

The homepage samples this mask on a canvas sphere. The arcs are illustrative coordinates, not live GressPay payments.
